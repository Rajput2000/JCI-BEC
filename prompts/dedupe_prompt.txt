following names were extracted via OCR from one or more screenshots of the
same meeting's participant list, so some entries may be the exact same
person written slightly differently (inconsistent capitalization, extra or
missing whitespace, a minor OCR misread of one or two characters).

Your task: merge only entries that are clearly the SAME spelling of the same
person's name with superficial formatting/OCR noise. Do NOT merge names that
could plausibly belong to different people, and do NOT merge based on
nicknames, initials, or shortened forms — those are handled in a later step
against an official roster, not here.

Rules:
- Do not invent, fabricate, or add any name not present in the input list.
- Do not drop a name unless merging it into a near-identical duplicate
  already in the list.
- When merging duplicates, keep the cleanest/most complete-looking spelling.
- Preserve the relative order of first appearance for each unique name.
- Return ONLY JSON of the form {{"unique_names": ["Name One", "Name Two"]}} —
  no other text.
